Sarfy is a simple, privacy-minded expense tracker that helps you stay on top of everyday spending. Record an expense in seconds, review your spending at a glance, and keep your budget organized in one place.

FEATURES
• Log expenses with an amount, category, and optional note.
• Review daily, weekly, and monthly totals, recent transactions, and a breakdown by category.
• Set a monthly spending limit and follow your progress, remaining budget, or any amount over the limit.
• Choose a currency symbol and personalize the app with color themes.
• Use the interface in Arabic or English, with right-to-left layout for Arabic.
• Store expense records locally on your device for offline access.
• Export a CSV report for use in spreadsheet apps.

Sarfy is designed for quick, straightforward personal expense tracking. It is not a banking app and does not connect to financial accounts.
